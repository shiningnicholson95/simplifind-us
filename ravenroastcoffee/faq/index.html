Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question? Here are the ones we hear most.

Visiting

What are your hours?
Our hours change with the seasons. Call us at (970) 586-4326 and we’ll let you know what today looks like.
Where exactly are you located?
164 East Elkhorn Ave, Unit I, inside the George Hix Riverside Plaza in downtown Estes Park, CO 80517. We’re a block off the main strip, by the river. Get directions.
Can I sit down and stay a while?
Yes. There’s indoor and outdoor seating, and table service is available. Lots of guests work here with the free Wi-Fi and outlets.
Is there parking nearby?
Yes. There’s free street parking, a free lot and paid lots nearby, and usually plenty of spaces.
Do you take reservations?
Yes, we accept reservations. Call (970) 586-4326 to book.
Is there live music?
Yes. We host live music and live performances. Call to hear what’s coming up.

Drinks

Do you have options besides coffee?
Plenty: specialty teas, chai, matcha, cocoa, steamers, spiced cider, lemonade, Arnold Palmers, Italian sodas, smoothies and mocktails.
How large is the tea selection?
Large. We carry black, green, herbal, white and oolong teas, from English Breakfast and Earl Grey to Marrakesh Mint, chamomile and rooibos blends. You can buy any loose-leaf tea by the bag.
Do you have dairy alternatives?
Yes, several, including oat and macadamia milk.
Are there sugar-free options?
Yes. We have a full sugar-free syrup lineup, including chocolate, French vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, lavender and peppermint.
What do drinks usually cost?
Most drinks cost between about $3 and $7, depending on size and flavor. See the menu for the full price list.
What do all the drink names mean?
We keep a plain-language guide on the coffee terms page, covering everything from cortado to tisane.

Food

Do you have food?
We have cookies, lemon bars, pastries and other baked goods at the counter, plus small plates and quick bites.
Do you have vegan or gluten-free food?
Yes. We have vegan and vegetarian options and a selection of gluten-free baked goods.
Is there a kids’ menu?
Yes, including kids cocoa.
Do you cater?
Yes. Send us a catering request or call (970) 586-4326 to talk through what you need.

Accessibility & pets

Is the shop wheelchair accessible?
Yes. The entrance, parking lot, restroom and seating are all wheelchair accessible.
Are dogs allowed?
Yes. Dogs are welcome inside and outside.
Is the shop family-friendly?
Yes. We’re family-friendly and LGBTQ+-friendly, and a trans safe space.

Orders & payment

What payment methods do you take?
Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments.
Can I order takeout over the phone?
Give us a call at (970) 586-4326 and we’ll tell you what we can have ready for you.
Is delivery available?
Yes. We offer delivery as well as dine-in and takeout.
Do you sell gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards are available at the counter, or call (970) 586-4326.
