Coffee Terminology

Every word on our boards in plain language, so you can order exactly what you want.

Espresso basics

Espresso
A small, concentrated shot of coffee pulled under pressure. It’s the base of most drinks on the board.
Shot
One serving of espresso. Add extra shots to any drink for more caffeine and a stronger flavor.
Ristretto
A shorter shot pulled with less water, so it’s sweeter and more concentrated.
Lungo
A longer shot pulled with more water, so it’s milder and slightly more bitter.
Crema
The pale golden foam on top of a fresh espresso shot.
Shot in the Dark
A cup of house coffee with a shot of espresso added.

Milk drinks

Latte
Espresso with steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. It’s the easiest drink to add flavors to.
Cappuccino
Espresso with equal parts steamed milk and thick foam, so it’s lighter and airier than a latte.
Flat White
Espresso with steamed milk and very little foam. Think of it as a stronger, silkier latte.
Cortado
Espresso cut with a small amount of warm milk, served short.
Macchiato
Espresso “marked” with a spot of milk or foam.
Mocha
A latte with chocolate added. Ours use Ghirardelli chocolate or white chocolate.
Steamer
Steamed milk with a flavor syrup and no coffee, so it’s caffeine-free.
Microfoam
Finely textured steamed milk that pours smooth. We use it for lattes and flat whites.

Brewed coffee

House Coffee
Our standard drip coffee, brewed fresh through the day.
Americano
Espresso topped with hot water. It’s about as strong as drip coffee but tastes different.
Cold Brew
Coffee steeped in cold water for many hours. It’s smooth, low in acid and naturally sweeter.
Iced Coffee
Coffee brewed hot, chilled and poured over ice. It tastes brighter than cold brew.
Frozen Latte
A latte blended with ice into a thick, cold drink.

Tea & tisanes

Tisane
A caffeine-free herbal infusion such as chamomile, peppermint or rose hips. Often called herbal tea.
Loose Leaf
Whole tea leaves brewed without a bag, for fuller flavor. You can buy any of ours by the bag.
Chai
Black tea brewed with warming spices. We serve it as a latte with steamed milk.
Matcha
Stone-ground green tea powder whisked into the drink, so you consume the whole leaf.
Rooibos
A naturally caffeine-free red bush infusion from South Africa, sweet and nutty.
Oolong
A partly oxidized tea that sits between green and black in strength and flavor.
Pu’erh
An aged, fermented tea that’s deep, earthy and smooth.
Maté
A South American infusion with a clean, grassy taste and natural caffeine.

Cold & non-coffee

Italian Soda
Sparkling water with flavor syrup over ice.
Italian Cream Soda
An Italian soda finished with cream, for a smoother, softer drink.
Arnold Palmer
Half iced tea, half lemonade.
Mocktail
A layered non-alcoholic drink made with our syrups, juices and sparkling water.
Spiced Cider
Hot apple cider with warming spices. Caffeine-free.
Smoothie
A blended fruit drink in mango, strawberry, peach, cherry raspberry or blackberry.

Customizing your order

Dairy Alternative
Non-dairy milk. We carry several, including oat and macadamia.
Sugar-Free Syrup
Flavor syrup sweetened without sugar. Most of our flavors come in a sugar-free version.
Half Caff
Half regular, half decaf, for less caffeine without giving up the drink.
Extra Hot / Kid Temp
Milk steamed hotter than usual, or cooler so it’s safe for children.
Add-In
An extra stirred into a drink or smoothie: peanut butter, yogurt, banana, protein powder or CBD powder.
Small / Medium / Large
Our three sizes. Prices on the menu are listed for each size.
