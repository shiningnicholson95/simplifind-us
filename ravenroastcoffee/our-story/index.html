Our Story

A small coffee shop with a lot of personality, right in the middle of downtown.

Est. 2013

Coffee with character, in the town we call home

Raven’s Roast is a small coffee shop in the George Hix Riverside Plaza on East Elkhorn Avenue, right in the middle of downtown Estes Park.

Locals stop in on the way to work and visitors find us on the way to the park. Everyone gets the same thing: a good cup and a friendly hello. Stay a while, or take it with you up the mountain.

We’re proudly locally owned, women-owned and LGBTQ+-owned, and we want everyone who walks in to feel at home.

What makes us, us

Made to order

Every drink is made when you order it, with your milk and your flavor.

Community

Locals and visitors side by side, plus live music and local art on the walls.

Well reviewed

4.6 stars across 535+ Google reviews.

Easy to find

Downtown on East Elkhorn Avenue, by the river.

A serious tea wall

About 70 loose-leaf teas and tisanes, sold by the cup or by the bag.

Everyone’s welcome

Family-friendly and LGBTQ+-friendly, a trans safe space, and dogs are welcome too.
