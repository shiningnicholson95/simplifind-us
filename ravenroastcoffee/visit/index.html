    What to expect

    Service

    Dine-in, takeout, delivery, table service and catering.

    Highlights

    Great coffee, a great tea selection, a fireplace, and live music and performances.

    Food

    Quick bites, small plates and desserts, with vegan, vegetarian and kids’ options.

    Atmosphere

    Casual, cozy and quiet, and a good place to work on a laptop. Free Wi-Fi.

    Accessibility

    Wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking, restroom and seating.

    Parking

    A free lot, free street parking and paid lots nearby, and usually plenty of spaces.

    Pets

    Dogs are allowed inside and outside.

    Payments

    Credit cards, debit cards and tap-to-pay on your phone.

    Crowd

    Family-friendly and LGBTQ+-friendly, a trans safe space, and lots of visitors.

    Reservations

    We accept reservations. Call (970) 586-4326 to book a table.

    Popular for

    Breakfast, brunch, dessert, solo visits and getting some work done.

    Locally owned

    An independent shop that’s women-owned and LGBTQ+-owned.

    While you’re here

    An easy first or last stop downtown

    We’re steps from the river and the shops along Elkhorn Avenue. Fuel up before Rocky Mountain National Park, or warm up after.
