Our story

Locally owned, by the river, since 2013

Raven’s Roast is a small, women-owned and LGBTQ+-owned coffee shop tucked into the George Hix Riverside Plaza on East Elkhorn Avenue. Locals stop in on the way to work and visitors stop on the way to the park. Everyone gets a good cup and a friendly hello.

  • Raven-themed, cozy, a little bit gothic
  • Steps from the river and the Elkhorn shops
  • Free Wi-Fi and outlets if you need to get some work done
  • Live music and performances, so call to hear what’s on

Quick answers

Can I sit down and stay a while?
Yes. There’s indoor and outdoor seating, and table service is available. Lots of guests work here with the free Wi-Fi and outlets.
Is there parking nearby?
Yes. There’s free street parking, a free lot and paid lots nearby, and usually plenty of spaces.
Are dogs allowed?
Yes. Dogs are welcome inside and outside.
