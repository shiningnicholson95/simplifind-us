The Beginning

Born from two times of day we love most.

Kafe Krave started with a simple observation: the best parts of a day are the unhurried ones. The first quiet coffee before the city wakes. The last drink as the lights go low. We built one room to hold both.

By morning we're a neighborhood coffee shop — pour-overs, espresso, fresh pastry. By night the same space softens into a cocktail bar, where San Antonio gathers to slow down together.

Our Philosophy

"We don't chase trends. We chase the feeling of a room you don't want to leave — and we build everything backward from there."
