The Gallery

A space shaped by vision, movement, and material.

ARTSHO is an art gallery showcasing a striking collection of large-scale works by David Stainback. His bold oil paintings showcase captivating color compositions and are inspired by the urban environment, viewed through a distinctive designer's eye.

Every piece on view is chosen for the same quality: a stillness that rewards a second, longer look.
