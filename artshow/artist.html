About the Artist

A designer's eye, turned on the city.

David Stainback is the featured artist of ARTSHO Galleries, where his large-scale oil paintings anchor the collection. His work approaches the urban environment — architecture, storefronts, signage, reflections, the everyday geometry of a city block — with an eye for scale, proportion, and color more often associated with graphic and industrial design than traditional landscape painting.

Rather than depicting a place in full, Stainback isolates a single fragment of it: a window, a corner, a stretch of shadow. The result is a body of work that reads at once as representational and abstract — a recognizable urban moment, distilled into a bold, confident composition of color and edge.

Process & Materials

Oil on canvas, at scale.

Each piece begins as a fragment of the city, studied and simplified until only its essential shapes and color relationships remain. Stainback then builds that composition up in oil on canvas, working large — many pieces are painted at 40 inches square or bigger — so the color reads with the same confidence up close as it does across a room.

The result is a collection meant to be experienced in person: paintings with enough scale and surface to anchor a room, and enough restraint to reward a slow, close look.

Every painting begins with a single question: what happens when a city block is reduced to color, edge, and light?

— ARTSHO Galleries, on David Stainback's practice
