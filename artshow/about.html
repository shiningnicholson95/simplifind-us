About Us

The story behind ArtSho

A gallery built around a simple idea: that everyday moments, painted with care, deserve a room of their own.

Our History

A space shaped by vision, movement, and material.

ARTSHO Galleries opened its doors on Market Street in West Chester with a single mission: to give David Stainback's large-scale, contemporary oil paintings a home outside the usual white-cube gallery format.

Rather than chasing trends, the collection centers on the urban environment — cityscapes, storefronts, architecture, and the corners of daily life — reframed through Stainback's designer's eye into bold color compositions.

Today the gallery continues that founding idea, pairing Stainback's new work with historic pieces from the late E. H. Suydam, and opening its rooms to the community for exhibitions and private events.

Our Philosophy

Still moments, closely observed

We believe the most interesting subjects are often the ones we walk past every day. Our curation favors patience over spectacle — paintings that ask to be looked at slowly.
