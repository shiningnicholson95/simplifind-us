Remembering the Artist

A place in the permanent collection

E. H. Suydam's work has long been part of ARTSHO Galleries' holdings, offering visitors a historical counterpoint to David Stainback's contemporary practice. Where Stainback isolates fragments of today's city in bold, flattened color, Suydam's pieces bring an earlier eye for structure, line, and atmosphere to many of the same subjects — buildings, streets, and the quiet architecture of daily life.

The gallery keeps his work on view not as a footnote to the contemporary collection, but as a companion to it: two artists, working decades apart, drawn to the same idea that the built environment is worth looking at closely.

Two artists, a generation apart, both looking for the same thing: what a building or a street corner becomes once you stop to really see it.

— ARTSHO Galleries, on the permanent collection
