Private Events

Receptions, exhibitions, and creative gatherings

ArtSho's rooms are available to rent for private events of nearly any kind — from intimate receptions to small exhibitions and creative workshops. Surrounded by original paintings, guests get an atmosphere that a standard event space simply can't offer.

Every booking includes access to our current collection as a backdrop, flexible room layouts, and a team on hand to help the evening run smoothly.
