About the Appraisal

USPAP — the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice — is the national standard for licensed appraisers. Every McKoy Harris report is USPAP-compliant, which is what lenders, courts, government agencies, and the IRS expect.

An exterior-only report (Form 2055) values the property from the street and public records without entering the home. Interior reports (Form 1004 for one unit, Form 1025 for 2–4 units) include a full walk-through and floorplan sketch, and are required for most legal and lending uses.

Automated estimates never see the home, and a CMA is a pricing opinion prepared for marketing. A certified appraisal is an impartial, USPAP-compliant opinion of value by a licensed appraiser who inspects the property — and it carries weight with lenders, courts, and tax boards.
