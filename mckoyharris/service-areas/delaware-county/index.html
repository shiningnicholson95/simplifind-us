Local Knowledge

What Delaware County Clients Should Know

Countywide Reassessment

Delaware County completed a countywide reassessment that took effect for the 2021 tax year. Homeowners who believe their assessment is too high can appeal to the Delaware County Board of Assessment Appeals with a certified appraisal as evidence.

Estates & Divorce

The county courthouse and Register of Wills are in Media. Our court-ready reports support estate administration, equitable distribution, and buyout negotiations.
