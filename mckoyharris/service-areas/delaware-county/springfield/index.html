The Springfield Market

What Drives Home Values in Springfield

Because many Springfield homes share similar size and age, renovations, additions, and condition drive most of the value difference between them. That makes Springfield a common market for PMI removal and tax assessment appeals backed by a certified appraisal.

Every McKoy Harris appraisal is prepared by Antwan McKoy-Harris, a Pennsylvania Certified Residential Appraiser (License #RL140441), and follows USPAP — the standard expected by lenders, courts, and tax boards.

Springfield Appraisal FAQs

Yes. McKoy Harris Valuation provides certified residential appraisals throughout Springfield, covering Springfield Township, Delaware County (ZIP 19064).

Residential appraisals range from $450 (exterior-only) to $850+ (2–4 units), with single-family interior appraisals starting at $650. Complex or high-end properties are quoted individually.
