The Media Market

What Drives Home Values in Media

With the courthouse and Register of Wills in town, Media is the center of Delaware County estate and family-law matters. We regularly prepare court-ready appraisals for executors and attorneys handling Delaware County estates and equitable distribution cases.

Every McKoy Harris appraisal is prepared by Antwan McKoy-Harris, a Pennsylvania Certified Residential Appraiser (License #RL140441), and follows USPAP — the standard expected by lenders, courts, and tax boards.

Media Appraisal FAQs

Yes. McKoy Harris Valuation provides certified residential appraisals throughout Media, covering Media Borough and the surrounding townships of Upper Providence, Nether Providence, and Middletown (ZIP 19063).

Residential appraisals range from $450 (exterior-only) to $850+ (2–4 units), with single-family interior appraisals starting at $650. Complex or high-end properties are quoted individually.

Most inspections take about an hour, and the completed report is typically delivered within 12–14 business days of the inspection.
