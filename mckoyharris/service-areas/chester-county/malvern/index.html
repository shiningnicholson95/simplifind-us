Malvern Appraisal FAQs

Yes. McKoy Harris Valuation provides certified residential appraisals throughout Malvern, covering Malvern Borough and the surrounding townships of East Whiteland, Willistown, and Charlestown (ZIP 19355).

Residential appraisals range from $450 (exterior-only) to $850+ (2–4 units), with single-family interior appraisals starting at $650. Complex or high-end properties are quoted individually.

Most inspections take about an hour, and the completed report is typically delivered within 12–14 business days of the inspection.
