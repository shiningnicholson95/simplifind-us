Local Knowledge

What Chester County Clients Should Know

Estates & Probate

Chester County's courts and Register of Wills are in West Chester. Executors and attorneys rely on our date-of-death and retrospective appraisals for estate administration and inheritance tax filings.

Tax Assessment Appeals

Appeals go to the Chester County Board of Assessment Appeals. Most Pennsylvania counties set an annual filing deadline of August 1 — confirm the current date with the county and order your appraisal well ahead of it.
