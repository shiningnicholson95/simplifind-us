Local Knowledge

What Philadelphia Clients Should Know

Estates in Philadelphia

Philadelphia estates are handled by the Register of Wills at City Hall. Date-of-death appraisals establish value for estate administration and Pennsylvania inheritance tax.

Assessment Appeals

The Office of Property Assessment (OPA) sets assessed values, and appeals go to the Board of Revision of Taxes — typically due by the first Monday in October. A certified appraisal documents true market value.
