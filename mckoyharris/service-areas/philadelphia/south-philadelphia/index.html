What Drives Home Values in South Philadelphia

In South Philly, two rowhomes on the same block can differ dramatically in value depending on renovation quality, roof decks, finished basements, and parking. A careful interior inspection is the only way to capture those differences accurately.

Every McKoy Harris appraisal is prepared by Antwan McKoy-Harris, a Pennsylvania Certified Residential Appraiser (License #RL140441), and follows USPAP — the standard expected by lenders, courts, and tax boards.

South Philadelphia Appraisal FAQs

Yes. McKoy Harris Valuation provides certified residential appraisals throughout South Philadelphia, covering neighborhoods including East and West Passyunk, Pennsport, Point Breeze, Girard Estate, and Packer Park.

Residential appraisals range from $450 (exterior-only) to $850+ (2–4 units), with single-family interior appraisals starting at $650. Complex or high-end properties are quoted individually.

Most inspections take about an hour, and the completed report is typically delivered within 12–14 business days of the inspection.
