Local Knowledge

What Montgomery County Clients Should Know

Estates & Probate

Montgomery County's courthouse and Register of Wills are in Norristown. Our retrospective appraisals establish date-of-death value for estate administration and inheritance tax.

Tax Assessment Appeals

Appeals are heard by the Montgomery County Board of Assessment Appeals. A certified appraisal is the clearest evidence that an assessment exceeds market value.
