Meet Your Appraiser

Antwan McKoy-Harris

Founder · PA Certified Residential Appraiser

McKoy Harris Valuation was founded on a simple conviction: homeowners, lenders, and legal professionals across Greater Philadelphia deserve appraisals built on integrity — not rushed, not biased, and never one-size-fits-all.

Antwan holds a Pennsylvania Certified Residential Appraiser license and handles single-family, multi-family (2–4 unit), mixed-use, and vacant land assignments throughout Chester, Delaware, Philadelphia, and Montgomery counties.

Clients consistently mention the same things: a thorough walk-through, clear explanations of how value was determined, and a phone that actually gets answered.

The Process

From First Call to Final Report

1

Request & Consult

Submit your request online or call. We confirm the right report type for your purpose and quote your fee up front.

2

Agreement & Payment

Review and sign the engagement agreement, then pay securely through our CPACharge portal.

3

Property Inspection

Antwan inspects and measures the property, documenting condition, upgrades, and any deferred maintenance.

4

Market Research

Comparable sales, market trends, and neighborhood data are researched and verified.

5

Report Preparation

Your USPAP-compliant report is written and quality-reviewed.

6

Report Delivery

Delivered in PDF, XML, or ENV format — typically within 12–14 business days of the inspection.
