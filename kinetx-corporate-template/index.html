04 / Meet The Team

Dr. Gene Serafim, DC

Chiropractor & Kinesiologist • DACRB

Dr. Serafim treats the root cause of pain — not just the symptoms. Trained alongside both chiropractors and physical therapists, he blends adjustments, muscle activation, mobility work, and corrective exercise into a custom plan for every patient. He's certified in ConnectX, a Graston-like soft-tissue technique, and is one of only a few hundred chiropractors nationwide to hold the DACRB rehabilitation diplomate.
