Our Story

Treatment That Actually Holds

Too many people bounce between providers who only treat the symptom — a quick adjustment here, a massage there — and end up right back in pain a week later. Kinetx exists to break that cycle. We treat the root cause, then build the strength and movement to keep it from coming back.

That means blending what usually lives in separate offices: chiropractic, soft-tissue therapy, and progressive rehabilitation, all under one roof and one plan. The adjustment restores motion; the rehab makes it last. Together, they get you better faster and keep you that way.

Whether you're chasing a PR, training for a race, or just want to pick up your kids without your back screaming, we meet you where you are and build a clear path to where you want to be.

Meet The Doctor

Dr. Gene Serafim, DC

Chiropractor & Kinesiologist • Founder

Dr. Serafim has spent his career at the intersection of chiropractic, rehabilitation, and human movement. Trained alongside both chiropractors and physical therapists, he treats the root cause of pain — combining adjustments, muscle activation, mobility work, and corrective exercise into a plan built for each patient.

He holds the DACRB rehabilitation diplomate — an advanced certification earned by only a few hundred chiropractors nationwide — and is certified in ConnectX, a Graston-like soft-tissue technique. He also taught anatomy and physiology at the graduate level, bringing a deep understanding of the body to every treatment.
