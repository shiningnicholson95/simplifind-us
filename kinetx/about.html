My story

Treatment that actually holds

Too many people bounce between providers who only treat the symptom. A quick adjustment here, a massage there, and a week later they're right back in pain. I started Kinetx to break that cycle: find the root cause, treat it, then build the strength and movement to keep it from coming back.

That means bringing together things that usually live in separate offices. Chiropractic, soft-tissue therapy and progressive rehab, all under one roof and one plan. The adjustment restores motion; the rehab makes it last. Together they get you better faster and keep you that way.

I trained alongside both chiropractors and physical therapists, and I've taught anatomy and physiology at the graduate level. That background shapes every visit: I want you to understand what's going on in your body and leave knowing exactly what to do about it.

Whether you're chasing a personal best, training for a race, or just want to pick up your kids without your back complaining, I'll meet you where you are.
