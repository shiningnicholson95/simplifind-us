A note from Dr. Gene

Why I practice the way I do

Too many people bounce between providers who only treat the symptom. A quick adjustment here, a massage there, and a week later they're right back where they started. I wanted to break that cycle.

I trained alongside both chiropractors and physical therapists, and I've taught anatomy and physiology at the graduate level. So when you come in, I'm looking at how your whole body moves, not just where it hurts. Then I put together a plan that blends adjustments, soft-tissue work, mobility and corrective exercise: the adjustment restores motion, and the rehab makes it last.

Whether you're training for a race or just want to pick up your kids without your back complaining, I'll meet you where you are and give you a clear path to where you want to be.
