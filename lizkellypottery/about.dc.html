About

Liz Kelly

Originally from Raleigh, NC, she's brought her collective travels, education and life experience back home to a pottery studio practice. Passionate about expressive home goods and intentional consumerism, she grounds her fun wares in functionality.

Traditional craft and contemporary design inspire her work — functional home goods that connect people with material and experience, earthy and elegant with a modern approach to handmade pottery. The pieces accent your home, inspire ritual, and encourage hospitality.

Her degree in Design Studies from the renowned NC State College of Design grounds her studio practice in the craft origins of design — the maker/designer's legacy of functional beauty — and shapes a values-driven approach to her work and business.
