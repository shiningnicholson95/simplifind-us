The Studio

A working studio, not a showroom.

Liz Kelly has been throwing pottery since she was 16, and today runs her studio practice out of Raleigh's historic Boylan Heights neighborhood. A graduate of NC State's College of Design, she makes every piece from start to finish — throwing, glazing, and firing wares that pair earthy, functional forms with playful hand-applied decals.

No two pieces come out quite the same, and that's the point — each one carries the mark of the hand that made it.
