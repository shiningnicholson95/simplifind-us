Getting here

Parking

Several public parking garages and lots serve La Villita and the nearby Convention Center, all within a short walk.

River Walk access

La Villita sits directly on the San Antonio River Walk — stroll in from the river level or from street level above.

Accessibility

La Villita offers accessible routes throughout the village. Please call ahead and we'll be glad to help you plan your visit.

Historic setting

We're housed in the historic Florian House — a landmark building within La Villita Artisan Village.

While you're in La Villita

Make a day of it — our corner of San Antonio is surrounded by history, shops and the River Walk.

La Villita Artisan Village

A historic arts village of galleries, studios and shops just outside our door.

The San Antonio River Walk

World-famous riverside paths, dining and boat tours steps from the gallery.

Downtown landmarks

The Alamo, HemisFair and the Convention Center are all within easy walking distance.

Visitor questions

Do I need a ticket?+

No — admission is free and no reservation is needed. Just stop by any day between 10am and 6pm.

Can I buy art to take home?+

Yes. Most pieces are available for purchase in the gallery, and you can also shop a selection in our online store.

Is the gallery good for groups or schools?+

Absolutely — students, families and community groups are welcome. For larger groups, give us a call ahead at 210·226·8752.
