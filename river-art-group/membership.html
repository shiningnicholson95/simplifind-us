For artists

Become a member of River Art Group

We accept new members twice a year through a friendly two-step jury process. Local artists are encouraged to apply — we'd love to meet you.

Why join

Show in a landmark

Display and sell your work in the historic Florian House on the River Walk.

Join a community

Become part of 150+ artists supporting one another since 1947.

Compete & grow

Enter our annual members' competition and jury into additional media.

Reach visitors

Be seen by the tourists, collectors and locals who fill La Villita year-round.

How to apply

  1. 1

    Download the New Member Application

    Read it carefully so you understand our gallery rules and standards before applying.

  2. 2

    Email your completed application

    Send it to Membership Director Shelley Christ-Pizana at riverartgroup@riverartgroup.com, or mail it with a check (see the application for instructions).

  3. 3

    Pay the $25 non-refundable jury fee

    Pay securely online, or include a check with a mailed application.
