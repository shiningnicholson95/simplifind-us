Welcome

A gallery built and run by the artists themselves — since two small groups of San Antonio painters joined forces in 1947.

Every piece on our walls is the work of a juried member, and every day the gallery is open because a member is here to welcome you. The result is a living, ever-changing collection — and a genuine creative community in the heart of La Villita.
