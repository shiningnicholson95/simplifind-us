About the artist

Wendell Fuqua is a longtime member of River Art Group, working in the demanding tradition of woodcut and relief printmaking. Each piece begins as a hand-carved block, inked and pressed by hand to produce a limited edition of original prints.

His subjects often celebrate the landmarks and light of San Antonio — from the Tower of the Americas glowing at dusk to the river and skyline that draw visitors to La Villita.

Artist statement

"There's nothing quite like pulling a finished print off the block for the first time. I want each one to hold the warmth and color of this city — the places people here know by heart."
