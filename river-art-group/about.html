Since 1947

Offering unique art and cultivating creative community.

For more than seventy-five years, River Art Group has been a home for San Antonio's artists — and an open door for everyone who loves their work.

How we began

River Art Group was formed during the summer of 1947, when two small groups of San Antonio artists shared the notion of mounting a combined art show on the newly refurbished River Walk. That first collaboration grew into one of the city's most enduring art organizations.

Today, River Art Group has over 150 member artists from San Antonio and the surrounding communities, working in a wide variety of media — acrylic, oil, watercolor, pen and ink, pastel, photography, clay, metal, wood, and fine art reproductions. Many artists are juried in for multiple categories.

We are fortunate to have remarkably talented members, and every activity — including the daily operation of the gallery — is directed and carried out by them. The hard work and dedication of our members over the decades is the foundation of everything we do.

Our mission

To offer unique, original art to the public and to cultivate a thriving creative community for local artists in San Antonio.

Our vision

A San Antonio where working artists have a welcoming place to show and sell their work — and where visitors can always discover something new and handmade.

Community impact

Beyond the gallery, we run a yearly members' competition and host RiverArtober — a free outdoor festival — plus smaller events that bring art to the River Walk all year.

Our home

The historic Florian House

Our gallery is housed in the historic Florian House, within La Villita Artisan Village along the world-famous San Antonio River Walk. With its sunny stucco walls, turquoise doors and shaded courtyard, the building is a landmark in its own right — and the perfect setting for a gallery that has always belonged to the community.

Run by artists

A volunteer board and member-run gallery

River Art Group is led by a volunteer board of member artists and powered by every member's contribution — each takes a turn staffing the gallery so our doors can stay open every day of the week. It's a model that has sustained us since 1947.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an admission fee?+

Never — visiting the gallery is always free. Stop in any day of the week between 10am and 6pm.

Can I buy the art on display?+

Yes. Most pieces in the gallery are for sale, and you can also browse and purchase a selection online anytime through our online shop. Our collection changes constantly, so there's always something new.

How are the artists selected?+

Members are juried in to ensure work meets gallery standards, and many artists jury into multiple media categories. We welcome new members twice a year — see our membership page for details.

Where exactly are you located?+

In the historic Florian House at 418 Villita St, Suite #1400, San Antonio, TX 78205 — inside La Villita Artisan Village, just off the San Antonio River Walk.

How can I support River Art Group?+

Visit and buy local art, follow us on social media, attend RiverArtober, or make a donation to help us keep supporting San Antonio artists.
