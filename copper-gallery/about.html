Our Story

A little shop devoted to the craft of copper.

Copper Gallery sits inside La Villita Historic Arts Village, one of San Antonio's oldest neighborhoods — a cluster of restored brick buildings where local artisans have sold their work for generations. Our red-trimmed storefront under the hanging Copper Gallery — Unique Gifts sign has become a favorite stop for visitors wandering between The Alamo and the River Walk.

We're proud to be a women-owned business. What we sell is simple: copper we love. Hand-hammered vessels, wearable jewelry and home art — pieces with real weight, a warm glow, and the beautiful, living patina that only copper develops over time.
