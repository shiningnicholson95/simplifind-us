A San Antonio landmark

Unique gifts you won't find anywhere else.

Tucked into the brick storefronts of La Villita Historic Arts Village — just steps from The Alamo and the River Walk — Copper Gallery is a women-owned shop devoted to the timeless craft of copper. Every piece is chosen for its character: the glow of hammered metal, the depth of a hand-applied patina, the quiet weight of something made by hand.
