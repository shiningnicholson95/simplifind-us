우리 찻집 · Our Teahouse

More than a boba shop —
it's a little home.

Woo Ri means "us" in Korean — and that's the feeling owner Woo set out to create when she opened the shop on Broadway. What began as a love of late-night boba runs and her family's kimbap recipes became a little corner of San Antonio where everyone's welcome.

Every first-timer snaps a Polaroid for the memento wall. Regulars come for the kind staff, the K-drama on the TV, and the local kitties out front. Come once, and you'll see why folks keep coming back.
